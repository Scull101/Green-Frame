Service 02

Commercial Video
Production in the UAE

High-production campaigns for product launches, advertising and corporate communications — planned, shot and edited for brands that need their video to perform, not just look good.

Commercial video work is where scripting, logistics and production value have to line up on the day — there's no reshoot budget when you're chasing light in the desert or coordinating a launch event. Our flagship example is the Uber Dunes launch campaign, produced with First & Ten Productions: fast-moving vehicles, shifting light and blowing sand, delivered as a full campaign asset set.

We also produce automotive and product-focused commercial work — from dealership campaigns to lifestyle product films — built for TV, web and paid social distribution.

  • Concept and scripting before production begins
  • On-location and studio shoots, including complex logistics (desert, motion, events)
  • Edits delivered cut for web, social and paid media formats
  • Full-service: one point of contact from brief to delivery

See the full breakdown in our Uber Dunes case study, or book this as a one-off shoot through the ONE TAKE package.
